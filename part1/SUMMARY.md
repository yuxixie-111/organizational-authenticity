# What the About page says

These pages are short, and most of them barely change. We could read 265 of 450 (59%). Banks were the easiest to find (74%). Energy was the hardest (36%), usually because the archive has no About page in the early years. Another 64 captures turned out to be a menu once the navigation was stripped. Six downloads failed when the archive cut us off: Broadcom 2018 to 2021, Meta 2022, and NVIDIA 2019. A missing year stays missing.

The typical page is 355 words. Where two years in a row both exist (210 pairs), the overlap in the middle is 95%. Still, 75 pairs (36%) change enough to count as a rewrite.

Customers, community, and employees show up on about two-thirds of pages. Innovation's share of the wording falls from about a fifth in 2016, 2017, and 2018 to 7% in 2024. Environment goes the other way, from 10% to 16%. Inclusion gets named more often after 2020: 27% of pages in 2020, 52% in 2022, then back around 39%. Even then it is a side topic. Its share of the wording stays under 9%.

The website and the proxy do not move together. Inclusion language in the proxy jumps in the 2021 filing season. On the About page, mentions rise in 2021, peak in 2022, and never take over the page. Wells Fargo's About page is almost the same every year, with inclusion stuck near a tenth of the themes. The swell and retreat happened in the filing, not on the website.

Fourteen pages hit none of the ten themes. Apple's early About page is a street address. It stays that way. Replacing it with the leadership page would have made up a values statement Apple did not publish.
