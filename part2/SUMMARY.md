# What the proxy emphasizes

Inclusion language jumps in the 2021 proxy season, a year after the protests, and then cools. The practice stays.

Mentions run at 0.37 per 1,000 words in 2016, 0.72 in 2019, and 0.76 in 2020. The 2020 proxies were mostly drafted as the protests began, and they barely moved. The 2021 proxies, the first full season afterward and the first under the SEC's human-capital rule, jump to 1.41. By 2024 the rate is 1.10, still about three times 2016. Consumer firms fall from 1.80 to 1.06. Financials fall from 2.16 to 1.48 and stay the loudest. Healthcare barely cools. Energy stays lowest, from 0.26 to 0.70.

The concrete disclosure does not cool with the wording. A proxy counts if it has a representation figure, a pay-equity statement, or diversity tied to pay: 54% in 2016, 74% in 2020, 92% in 2021, 94% in 2024. Broadcom hits that in one year out of nine. Halliburton, EOG, and Thermo Fisher hit it in about a third. Berkshire has no DEI practice through 2020, then a representation disclosure from 2021 on. Wells Fargo goes from 0.61 per 1,000 words in 2016 to 3.99 in 2021 and back to 0.99 in 2023, with pay equity on the page almost every year. The firm got louder, then quieter. The number stayed.

Climate language keeps rising through the years people expected an ESG retreat. Environment mentions go from 0.59 per 1,000 words in 2016 to 1.10 in 2020 and 2.26 in 2022, and stay near 2.3 through 2024. An emissions target or climate-linked pay goes from 38% of proxies to 72% and then 96% or higher. Energy talks about this most, about 4.7 mentions per 1,000 words in 2022. The sharper change is at the banks: 1.52 in 2021 to 3.38 in 2023.

Otherwise it is the same document. Median similarity to last year's proxy is 0.97. Only 20 of 395 pairs look like a rewrite. Tone sits near 0.78 the whole time. The template does not change. The emphasis does, which is why the per-1,000 counts are the thing to watch.

Three years are blank on purpose. Apple has no 2018 filing date. ExxonMobil's 2021 file is contest letters, not a definitive proxy. Starbucks' next proxy is January 2025. Borrowing the neighboring year would have been tidier and less honest.
