# How close the two documents are

The score averages two questions. Do the About page and the proxy stress the same themes? And when the About page actually features a theme the proxy can back up, is the practice there? The scale runs from 0 to 100. If either document is missing, or the About page names none of the themes, the score is blank. That is a missing comparison, not a failing grade.

250 of 450 firm-years get a score. The average is 37, the middle score is 36, and they spread widely (standard deviation 22, from about 1.5 to 94). The average moves from 23 in 2016 to 47 in 2021, then 36 in 2023 and 41 in 2024. Theme overlap rises from 0.31 to about 0.47. The share of featured themes with a matching practice rises from 0.12 to 0.47 in 2021 and falls to 0.27 in 2023. DEI and climate boxes in the proxy stay near the ceiling after 2021. What slips in 2023 is the match between what the website is featuring and what the proxy actually does.

Length is not the score. Correlation with About-page length is −0.09, and with proxy length is 0.04. Dropping shareholder language, which a proxy has to include, raises average similarity from 0.41 to 0.47. The legal boilerplate was pulling the two sides apart. Turning the practice bonus off, or turning it way up, leaves the ranking almost unchanged (correlations 0.98 and 0.99).

Energy years that can be scored average 54. Consumer years average 29. Energy also has the fewest pages: 25 scored years, mostly after 2019. Valero runs from 66 to 94 on pages of about 200 words. Berkshire averages 70 for a different reason. Its homepage never features a theme we can check for a practice, so the number is just theme overlap, on a page under 200 words.

The firms picked before any score was computed land in the middle or toward the bottom. Salesforce averages 24. Nike is 35, Meta 39. Wells Fargo is 48, with all nine years present. Its About page barely changes, and both documents talk about integrity. A firm writing about its own conduct can look aligned. The score says the two documents stress the same things. It does not say those things are admirable.

The lowest averages are Charles Schwab, TJX, Broadcom, and Thermo Fisher, all under 18. After the short energy pages and Berkshire, the highest are BlackRock and Morgan Stanley, around 55 to 59.

Two limits belong next to the number. After 2021 almost every proxy has a DEI or climate practice, so that half of the score separates firms less, and the 2023 dip is about which themes the website features. A July About page is also paired with a proxy that was usually filed in the spring, about the year just finished. Same calendar year. Not the same moment.
